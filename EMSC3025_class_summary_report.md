## What changed in the class summary

### 1. Assessment scheme (the substantive change)

The written paper critique was **removed**. With students now able to generate written
reports using AI tools, a written critique no longer tells us much about their own
understanding, so the more authentic, in-person assessments were strengthened instead. The
15% it carried went to the oral critique (+10) and the exam (+5).

Final scheme (total = 100%):

| # | Task | Weight | Due | Return | Learning outcomes |
|---|------|--------|-----|--------|-------------------|
| 1 | Computing assignment 1 (Precipitation) | 10% | 2026-08-21 | 2026-09-04 | 1, 2, 3 |
| 2 | Computing assignment 2 (Soil moisture) | 15% | 2026-09-25 | 2026-10-09 | 1, 2, 3 |
| 3 | Computing assignment 3 (Groundwater) | 15% | 2026-10-23 | blank | 1, 2, 3 |
| 4 | Oral critique of a scientific paper | 20% | 2026-10-16 | blank | 4, 5 |
| 5 | Final exam | 30% | blank | blank | 1, 2, 3, 5 |
| 6 | Tutorial participation | 10% | blank | blank | 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 |

- **Previous approved split:** written 10 / computing 40 / oral 10 / exam 40.
- **New split:** computing 40 / oral 20 / participation 10 / exam 30.
- Tutorial participation is graded on **engagement, not attendance** (0/1/2 per tutorial,
  best 8 count), assessed in the Wednesday two-hour tutorial.

### 2. Assessment dates (converted to YYYY-MM-DD)

The form requires `YYYY-MM-DD or blank`; week references failed validation. Dates were
derived from the Sem 2 2026 teaching calendar (start Mon 27 Jul; six teaching weeks, then a
two-week break, then six more; teaching ends Fri 30 Oct):

| Teaching week | Dates | | Teaching week | Dates |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W4 | 17–21 Aug | | W9 | 5–9 Oct |
| W6 | 31 Aug–4 Sep | | W10 | 12–16 Oct |
| *(break* | *7–18 Sep)* | | W11 | 19–23 Oct |
| W7 | 21–25 Sep | | | |

Two deliberate choices to be aware of:
- **Due dates are set to the Friday of the relevant week** (no specific day was given).
- **Final-exam and tutorial-participation dates are left blank.** The exam date is set
  centrally through the examinations timetable (the EXAMINATION(S) note explains this), and
  participation runs across the whole semester rather than falling due on a single day.

### 3. SELT feedback question

"Has a change been made to the delivery of this course in response to feedback including
SELT?" → **YES**, with the description: *"In response to student feedback, the remote
sensing content is now integrated into the individual lecture topics across the course,
rather than being taught as a separate standalone block, so that students encounter
satellite observations alongside the related hydrological processes."*

### 4. Other tidy-ups

- **PARTICIPATION** section field filled with a note describing the tutorial-participation task.
- **Weekly Class Structure table:** the Week 12 cell no longer reads "Written paper critique
  due"; the oral critique was aligned to Week 10 to match that table.
